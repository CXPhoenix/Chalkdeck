# Sufficient context examples

The approved focus anchors determine these branches. Reason cites the supplied
purpose/expression and chosen focus. Deliver the actual selected style's complete
profile, not the branch metadata below. Missing optional fields do not delay delivery.
Multiple eligible anchors need one choice, not a universal winner.

```yaml
ai_concept:
  request: Introduce AI to high-school students
  branch: deliver_one_eligible
  eligible_styles: [wangxing]
measured_energy:
  request: Compare measured energy use
  branch: deliver_one_eligible
  eligible_styles: [gates]
multiple_anchors:
  request: Explain a product benefit and compare measured energy use
  branch: deliver_one_eligible
  eligible_styles: [jobs, gates]
```

AI introduction supplies concept explanation; Wangxing's viewpoint/visual focus is
eligible without a separate expression need. Measured energy comparison supplies
analytical evidence; Gates is eligible without a separate audience. The combined
product/evidence purpose supplies both Jobs and Gates anchors; either is valid.
Do not infer student expertise, teacher humor preferences or a venue.
