# Gates adjustment rule record

ID: `gates.presentation_preferences`; classification: adjustable. Evidence class:
project synthesis / user-selected preference, not an official Gates method or preset.
Basis: approved non-core boundary and [core/source record](core-rule.md), whose
G1/G3 support analytical relationships without establishing mandatory density.
Reuse their public-text/historical-example reading limits, no new visual/timing
measurement or effectiveness claim.

Apply only to requested compatible appearance or evidence-expression preferences.
State actual accepted choices and applicable conditions in Markdown and constraint.
Whitespace and reduced decoration are individually compatible, not an automatic
combined preset. Apply either only when requested/accepted; example preferences
are not caller preferences. Retaining the core is a condition, not acceptance of
an extra appearance change.
Evidence, quantities or system elements relate to a stated comparison/system point;
retain necessary labels/units/assumptions/qualifications and their relationship to
the evidence. Optional record absent without applicable preference; no default
white-space ratio, chart count or decoration threshold.

Compatible: more whitespace/fewer decorations around labeled A/B average-response
comparison, units/shared test assumptions/repeats/test-specific limit intact.
Conflicting: delete necessary metric labels/units/assumptions/limits and turn the
numbers into disconnected decoration. These are guidance judgments, not new
calculations, restructuring or rendering instructions.
