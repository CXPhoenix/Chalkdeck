# Jobs core/source record

## Rule identity

- Rule ID: `jobs.single_focus`.
- Classification: core.
- Evidence class: project synthesis grounded in recorded keynote observations and
  official benefit expression; not an official rule name or creator quotation.

## Required propositions and applicability

1. Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point.
2. Text/visual elements support that point, rather than independent competing claims.

Concise text and relevant visual evidence clarify this relationship. Several objects
can serve one comparison; count alone does not determine compliance. The alternatives
(display, benefit, reveal, comparison) need not all occur at once. A generic theme,
black background or product photo without a focal relationship is insufficient.
Preserve supplied conditions and qualifications needed to interpret the focal
point; for a comparison these can include shared test conditions and limits on
applicability. These are guidance constraints, not content transformation steps.

## Evidence basis and reading limits

**J1 — Recorded keynote observation.** The Archive's 2007 Macworld excerpt describes
an introduction culminating in one device and a comparison involving multiple
phones. This motivates focus supported by related elements; it is not a universal
one-object prohibition or proof of a whole-deck formula.
Source: [Steve Jobs Archive, Make Something Wonderful](https://book.stevejobsarchive.com/).
Reading scope: the Macworld 2007 excerpt and bracketed screen/stage descriptions,
rechecked as web text on 2026-10-03. The source states it is edited/excerpted.
No complete keynote playback or slide-by-slide visual/timing measurement was done.

**J3 — Official benefit expression.** Apple's iPod launch release links capacity to
portable listening utility. This informs understandable benefit focus; a release
is not evidence of a slide layout or proof that all numbers must avoid tables.
Source: [Apple, Apple Presents iPod (2001)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/10/23Apple-Presents-iPod/).
Reading scope: launch headline, product/benefit text and Jobs quotation, rechecked
2026-10-03. No layout inference is treated as direct source evidence.

The complete supporting-elements relationship is the project's practical synthesis,
not a claim that either source states this core rule verbatim. No fixed word count,
font size, object count, timing, image ratio, universal chart/table ban or mandatory
buildup/reveal/demo/recap sequence is established. No general effectiveness claim
across audiences is made.

## Boundary examples

**Compatible:** Four products and a concise comparison label support one stated
comparison point. Their colors can vary while every element retains that relationship;
necessary test-condition notes remain readable supporting information. Four is an
illustrative example, not a threshold.

**Conflicting:** Several unrelated claims receive equal primary status, with no
shared display, benefit, reveal or comparison focus. A black background or larger
product photo does not repair the missing relationship.

These examples judge guidance statements, not rendered slides or a new
content transformation. See [adjustment record](adjustments.md) for requested preferences.
