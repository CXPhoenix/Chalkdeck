# English guidance example

Steve Jobs style: jobs.single_focus. Products A, B, C and D support one stated battery-life comparison. Their text and visuals support that shared point, not competing claims. A colored background is accepted; preserve common test conditions and limitations.

The accepted preferences use `jobs.presentation_preferences`.

These rules are source-led project synthesis, not creator quotations or official numerical formulas. Style constraints only; no content transformation.

```yaml
style_id: jobs
core_rules:
- rule_id: jobs.single_focus
  constraint: Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point. Text and visual elements support that focus rather than independent competing claims. Products A, B, C and D may jointly support one battery-life comparison; preserve the common test conditions and limited applicability.
adjustable_rules:
- rule_id: jobs.presentation_preferences
  constraint: Use a colored background and products A, B, C and D for the same battery-life comparison, without competing focal claims; retain common test conditions and applicability limits.
```

The four products and colored background are accepted preferences for this example, not a universal object-count or color rule.
