## Required propositions and applicability

1. Concise viewpoint text and a visual representation communicate the same viewpoint
   or an explicitly named relationship.
2. The visual contributes to understanding that point, rather than decoration alone.

Name the correspondence so that it can be checked; use supplied audience context
when available. A picture with an unrelated caption does not satisfy the core.
Color and absence of humor may retain it; monochrome and memes are not mandatory.
The style is not restricted to spoken projection. For independently read material,
keep the viewpoint, visual relationship and needed qualifications understandable
without assuming an accompanying speaker. Projection likewise cannot remove
necessary conditions merely to simplify. Do not generalize a qualified viewpoint
beyond supplied circumstances. This is guidance, not a content-editing procedure.

## Boundary examples

concise viewpoint about division of work. No joke is needed. The team-specific
qualification remains readable, including when the material is read independently.

**Conflicting:** An unrelated decorative picture is attached to a caption without
helping readers understand the viewpoint or a named relationship. Black-and-white
appearance does not repair that missing contribution.
